The Process

From submission to signed report

Every case follows the same rigorous four-step process — engineered for speed without compromising legal defensibility.

01
Secure Submission
Upload your file through our AES-256 encrypted portal. We compute a SHA-256 hash on receipt — establishing chain of custody from the moment it arrives.

Multi-Engine Analysis
Your submission runs independently through five specialist detection engines — video, audio, biological signal, pixel-level, and AI-generation analysis, all in parallel.

Human sign-off on every case
