Privacy Policy

Plain-language summary

Revlar LLC respects the sensitivity of the material you share with us. This summary explains, in plain terms, how we handle your information.

Information you provide through our contact form (such as your name, email, organization, and role), and any media or context you submit for a case.

How we use it

Solely to respond to your inquiry and to perform the verification you've requested. We do not sell your information, and we don't use submitted media for any purpose beyond your case.
