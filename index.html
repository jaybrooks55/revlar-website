The Process

From submission to signed report

Every case follows the same rigorous process — engineered for speed without compromising legal defensibility.

01
Secure Submission
Your file is uploaded through an encrypted portal. We compute a SHA-256 hash on receipt — establishing chain of custody from the moment it arrives.
Integrity locked on receipt
02
Multi-Engine Analysis
Your submission runs independently through multiple specialist detection engines — AI-generation, deepfake, and synthetic-voice analysis, in parallel.
No single point of failure
03
Analyst Review
A forensic analyst reviews every engine output, reconciles findings, and applies the contextual judgment that automated systems miss.
Human sign-off on every case
04
Signed Report
You receive a defensible verification report — clear verdict, confidence score, per-engine breakdown, chain-of-custody log, and SHA-256 hash. Ready for a case file, a newsroom, or a judge.
Court-ready in 24–72 hours
